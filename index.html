Why Cats?

Cats are really cute and adorable! You should consider getting a pet cat and care for it like it's your own child. All cats are good cats! Go forth and embrace becoming a proud parent of a cat!

Reasons you should get a cat:

  • Cats are cute!
  • Cats are the best!
  • Meow-mrrp-mrrrrrrp-purr

How to get a cat:

  1. Find a cat at a shelter first.
  2. Find a cat that is super friendly towards you.
  3. Take this cat home and give it yummy treats, litter box, water and food.

Congratulations! You have a cat!
